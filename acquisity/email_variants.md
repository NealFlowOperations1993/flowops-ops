# Acquisity Cold Email — 10 Variant A/B Test

Two niches: Recruiting/Staffing + Online Coaches (Eesha sub-niche TBD).
Each variant ships in BOTH niches, just swap the proof anchor + niche-specific pain.

All variants share:
- P.S. line: "Plan is free, implementation is separate"
- Booking link in signature
- Multi-touch sequence (emails 2-4 identical across variants)

---
